            Archive · Lore article 01

            The Chompy Chronicle

            The Chompy race did not begin on Aridaxx. Its story starts on Janira Prime, long before the violet world was made fit to live on.

            The Chompy age very slowly. A single ruler can reign for centuries, which is why so few names span so much of this history.

            1. 2.7 billion years ago
              The Merging

              On Janira Prime, the tribal leader Chompjak merged the scattered Chompy tribes into one people.

            2. 1 billion BC
              The Fall of the Republic

              Shakhak, a descendant of Chompjak, overthrew Arihak Jakizak, president of the Chompy Republic.

            3. 200 million BC
              The Fields of Steca

              Shakhak defeated the alien warlord Javek over the fields of Steca, a planet in the Steca Prime system. Steca lies close to Aridaxx, the terraformed world that had become the Chompy's new capital, in orbit around Sagittarius A*.

            4. After Steca
              The Bastion of Carax

              Javek fell, but his remnants held on. They still hold Carax, a bastion planet on the rim of Sagittarius A*, within reach of the Chompy capital.

            5. 1220–1240 AD
              The Chompjak Civil War

              The house of Chompjak split against itself. Its Janira Prime branch, rooted on the old homeworld, revolted against the Aridaxx branch that ruled from the capital.

              The war lasted twenty years. It ended in 1240 AD, when Jakal XV of the Janira Prime branch, father of Jakal Chompjak XVI, united the throne and forced his cousin Hakal of Aridaxx to resign.

            6. 1750 AD
              An Heir Is Born

              Hak Chompjak, son of Jakal Chompjak XVI, is born. He will become Sevirrus. His birth solves his father's lack of an heir.

            7. 1790 AD
              Before the Throne

              On Aridaxx, Sevirrus, born Hak Chompjak, stands one step below a throne he has already decided is his.

            8. 1790 AD
              The Assassination

              In the throne hall of Alphinos Keep, Sevirrus murders Damix Chompjak and Emperor Jakal Chompjak XVI, and takes the crown.

            Archive · Lore article 02

            Carax: A Chompy Mining Site

            A dark world of basalt and frost, cut through with rivers of molten ore, on the rim of Sagittarius A*.

            Carax was claimed by the Chompy as a mining site. The pull of the black hole cracks its crust from below, and the fissures run with molten ore that glows red even on the night side. That heat drives the rock's richest seams close to the surface, so Carax is mined where other worlds would need to be drilled.

            After the Fields of Steca, Javek's remnants held on at Carax and fortified it as their bastion. Over time, settlers came to live beside the old mine workings in domed outposts, working the seams in camouflage pressure suits and glass helmets against the thin, crimson air.

            1. Surface
              Basalt and frost

              Violet-grey plains of basalt and jagged ridges, with frost caps at the poles.

            2. Fissures
              Molten ore

              Cracks opened by the black hole's pull, glowing with molten ore. This is what made Carax worth mining.

            3. Sky
              Sagittarius A*

              The black hole fills much of the sky, its accretion disk lighting the planet a hot orange from one side.

            4. 1790 AD
              The massacre

              Five settlers and their leader were killed at the edge of the mine workings by the one called Sevirrus.

            Subject note · Sevirrus

            Aridaxx, the year 1790 AD. Under a violet sky that never fully darkens, something older than the planet is keeping its own time.

            Sevirrus, born Hak Chompjak, stands one step below a throne he has already decided is his. He holds no grudge against the people in his way, and he feels no loyalty to the ones who help him. Every alliance lasts exactly as long as it is useful.
